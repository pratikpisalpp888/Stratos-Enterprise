Stratos Enterprises Appraisal Guidelines
Confidential

Purpose:
The purpose of this document is to outline the guidelines for conducting performance appraisals at Stratos Enterprises. The appraisal process aims to provide a platform for employees to receive constructive feedback, set goals, and develop their skills in line with the company's objectives.

Scope:
This document applies to all employees of Stratos Enterprises, including those working on specific projects such as Project Helios, CloudEdge 3.0, and other initiatives.

Pre-Appraisal Preparation:
Before the appraisal meeting, the supervisor/manager shall:

1. Review the employee's performance records, including their goals, achievements, and areas for improvement.
2. Prepare relevant data and metrics to support the discussion, such as project timelines, customer satisfaction ratings, and team feedback.
3. Schedule a meeting with the employee at a mutually convenient time and ensure that they are aware of the agenda and expected outcomes.

Appraisal Meeting:
The appraisal meeting shall be conducted in a professional and respectful manner. The following points shall be covered:

1. Introduction and Review of Expectations:
   - Review the employee's job description and expectations.
   - Discuss the company's overall goals and how the employee contributes to its achievement.

2. Performance Review:
   - Highlight the employee's strengths and areas for improvement.
   - Discuss specific projects and initiatives, such as Project Helios and CloudEdge 3.0, and their contributions to these projects.
   - Address any performance issues or concerns raised by the employee or the team.

3. Goal Setting:
   - Set new goals and objectives aligned with the company's strategy and the employee's career development.
   - Establish key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure progress and achievement.

4. Development and Support:
   - Discuss opportunities for training, mentorship, and skill development.
   - Provide guidance on resources available to the employee, such as the Stratos Enterprises Learning Management System (LMS) and the employee assistance program (EAP).

Post-Appraisal Actions:
Following the appraisal meeting, the supervisor/manager shall:

1. Document the discussion and agreed-upon goals and objectives in the employee's performance record.
2. Provide a summary of the appraisal meeting to the employee, including any actions and next steps.
3. Schedule a follow-up meeting to review progress and provide feedback.

Performance Rating Scale:
Stratos Enterprises uses a 5-point rating scale to evaluate employee performance:

- 5: Exceptional - Meets all expectations and demonstrates exceptional performance.
- 4: Strong - Meets expectations and demonstrates strong performance.
- 3: Satisfactory - Meets expectations, but requires some improvement.
- 2: Needs Improvement - Fails to meet expectations and requires significant improvement.
- 1: Unsatisfactory - Fails to meet expectations and requires immediate attention.

Confidentiality:
All information discussed during the appraisal meeting shall remain confidential and in accordance with Stratos Enterprises' data protection policies (clause 4.5 of the Employee Handbook).

Amendments:
These guidelines shall be reviewed and updated annually, or as necessary, to ensure that they remain relevant and effective.
