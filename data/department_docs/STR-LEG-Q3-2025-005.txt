LEGAL DEPARTMENT QUARTERLY BUDGET REPORT Q3 2025
CONFIDENTIAL EXECUTIVE AND BOARD CIRCULATION ONLY


1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Legal Department operated within its approved Q3 2025 budget with total expenditure of 0.91 crore rupees against an allocated 0.95 crore rupees, resulting in a favorable variance of 4 lakh rupees which is 4.2 percent under budget. Controlled external counsel spend and lower than expected litigation activity contributed to this outcome.

Q3 priorities included enterprise contract reviews under Project Falcon, data protection compliance audits aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act also known as DPDP Act 2023, and vendor renegotiations tied to Project Helios infrastructure migration.

External legal counsel expenses declined due to early settlement of a vendor dispute with TechNova Solutions on July 18 2025, avoiding extended advisory fees projected at 6.5 lakh rupees. Final settlement cost amounted to 2.1 lakh rupees.

Compliance and Audit costs increased slightly due to a third-party data protection audit conducted by SecureLayer Labs between August 21 and 24 2025. Audit fees totaled 8.7 lakh rupees, exceeding the original estimate by 1.3 lakh rupees following scope expansion to include cross-border data transfer review.


3. KEY INITIATIVES

3.1 DPDP Act Compliance Readiness

Following the DPDP Act enforcement timeline update in June 2025, Legal allocated 14 lakh rupees in Q3 toward policy updates, consent framework redesign, and training sessions across Engineering and Sales. Approximately 312 employees completed mandatory compliance training by September 30 2025.

3.2 Enterprise Contract Negotiations

Legal reviewed and finalized 27 enterprise contracts in Q3, including the 3.8 crore rupees Zenith FinCorp SaaS agreement signed September 23 2025. Turnaround time averaged 5.2 business days, compared to 7.8 days in Q2 2025.

3.3 Vendor Renegotiation Project Helios

Legal supported renegotiation of infrastructure vendor clauses, reducing annual indemnity exposure by approximately 1.2 crore rupees through revised liability caps effective September 2025.


4. Q4 OUTLOOK AND RISK CONSIDERATIONS

The approved Q4 Legal budget is 1.02 crore rupees, factoring in anticipated regulatory advisory expenses and potential IP filings tied to the CloudEdge 3.0 product launch scheduled November 18 2025.

Finance recommends maintaining external counsel pre-approval thresholds at 3 lakh rupees per engagement and conducting a mid-quarter compliance expenditure review on November 20 2025.

Projected FY2025 Legal spend is 3.74 crore rupees against an annual allocation of 3.85 crore rupees, currently tracking within acceptable variance limits.
