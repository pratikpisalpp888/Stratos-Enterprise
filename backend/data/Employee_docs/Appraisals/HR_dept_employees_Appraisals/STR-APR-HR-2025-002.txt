Key Achievements

Tejas closed 18 technical hires in H1 2025 against a target of 14,
achieving 128 percent of his hiring goal. Roles closed included 8
backend engineers, 4 DevOps engineers, and 6 QA engineers across
Bangalore and Pune locations.

He reduced average time-to-offer for technical roles from 34 days in
H2 2024 to 24 days in H1 2025 by introducing a structured technical
screening template that reduced repeat interview rounds by 40 percent.

Tejas built a passive candidate pipeline of 67 pre-qualified senior

critical roles by approximately 2 weeks.

18 days per role.

He should improve documentation of candidate feedback and interview
notes in the ATS. Incomplete records for 7 candidates in Q1 2025
created compliance gaps during the HR audit conducted in April 2025.
