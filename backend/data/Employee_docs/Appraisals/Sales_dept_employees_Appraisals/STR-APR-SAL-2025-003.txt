Key Achievements

Farhan closed 3 enterprise accounts in H1 2025 with a combined contract
value of 2.94 crore rupees, achieving 122 percent of his H1 booking
target. His largest deal, a 1.38 crore rupees 2-year SaaS agreement
with Navkaar Logistics, was closed in 41 days from first meeting, the
fastest enterprise deal closure recorded in the Pune sales team in 2025.

He built and maintained a self-sourced pipeline of 7.8 crore rupees
entering Q3 2025, which represents 89 percent self-generated pipeline
against a team benchmark of 60 percent, demonstrating strong
prospecting discipline.

Farhan conducted 4 executive business review meetings with existing
enterprise accounts in Q2 2025 that resulted in two upsell opportunities
valued at 68 lakh rupees collectively, both currently in negotiation.


Areas of Improvement

Farhan should improve internal handoff documentation when transitioning
closed accounts to the Customer Success team. Three accounts handed off
in H1 2025 had incomplete implementation notes, causing onboarding delays
of 5 to 8 business days that received negative feedback from clients.

He needs to strengthen multi-threading within enterprise accounts. In two
accounts Farhan had a single champion contact and no executive sponsor
engagement, creating concentration risk that was flagged during the Q2
pipeline review.


Manager Comments

Karan Malhotra, Enterprise Sales Lead:

Farhan is one of the highest-performing enterprise AEs I have managed.
The Navkaar Logistics closure speed was exceptional and his pipeline

work on are post-sale handoff quality and multi-threading, both of which
are critical for long-term account health and renewal rates. I am
nominating him for the Enterprise Account Manager role in the next
promotion cycle.
