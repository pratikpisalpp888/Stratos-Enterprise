Key Achievements

Snehal generated 94 qualified leads in H1 2025 against a target of 70,
achieving 134 percent of her lead generation goal. Of these, 22 leads
converted to active pipeline opportunities with a combined value of
1.12 crore rupees.

She implemented a personalized outreach sequence for the BFSI vertical
in Q2 2025 that improved email response rates from 8 percent to 19
percent, which has since been adopted as a template by the inside sales
team for vertical-specific campaigns.

Snehal maintained an average call-to-connect rate of 31 percent in H1
2025, compared to the team average of 22 percent, attributed to her
structured call preparation and prospect research methodology.


Areas of Improvement

Snehal should develop stronger product knowledge depth, particularly
around technical differentiators of Stratos CloudEdge versus competing
platforms. Several prospect conversations stalled at the technical
qualification stage because she was unable to address infrastructure
specific questions without escalating to a Solutions Engineer.

She should work on shortening lead qualification cycles. Average time
from first contact to qualified handoff was 18 days in H1 2025 versus
a team benchmark of 12 days, suggesting room to tighten the discovery
process.


Manager Comments

Pallavi Nair, Inside Sales Lead:

Snehal has had an excellent H1 and her lead quality has been consistently

which is a real mark of initiative. My focus for her in H2 2025 is
developing technical depth and reducing qualification cycle time so she
is ready to step into a full Account Executive role by mid-2026.
