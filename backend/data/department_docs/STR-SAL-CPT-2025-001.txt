**Client Proposal Template**

**Project Reference:** Project Helios
**Client Name:** TechCorp Solutions
**Proposal Prepared By:** Neelima Reddy, Enterprise Sales Manager
**Date:** July 20, 2023

**Executive Summary:**
Stratos Enterprises is pleased to submit a proposal for the implementation of our CloudEdge 3.0 platform for TechCorp Solutions, a leading IT services company in India. The proposed solution aims to enhance TechCorp's cloud computing infrastructure, improve application performance, and reduce costs.

**Service Description:**

1. **CloudEdge 3.0 Implementation:** Our team will conduct a comprehensive assessment of TechCorp's current infrastructure, identify areas for improvement, and design a tailored CloudEdge 3.0 implementation plan. This will include migration of existing workloads, setup of new environments, and configuration of security and access controls.
2. **Managed Services:** Stratos Enterprises will provide 24/7 monitoring and management of the CloudEdge 3.0 environment, ensuring optimal performance, availability, and security. Our team will also perform regular security audits and provide recommendations for improvement.
3. **Training and Support:** Our experts will provide comprehensive training to TechCorp's IT staff on the administration and management of CloudEdge 3.0. We will also offer dedicated support through our help desk and escalation processes.

**Scope of Work:**

1. Assessment of existing infrastructure and identification of areas for improvement
2. Design and implementation of CloudEdge 3.0 platform
3. Migration of existing workloads to CloudEdge 3.0
4. Setup of new environments and configuration of security and access controls
5. Managed services and 24/7 monitoring
6. Training and support for TechCorp's IT staff

**Deliverables:**

1. Comprehensive assessment report highlighting areas for improvement
2. CloudEdge 3.0 implementation plan and design document
3. Training materials and documentation for CloudEdge 3.0 administration and management
4. Quarterly security audit reports and recommendations for improvement
5. Regular status updates and performance metrics

**Assumptions:**

1. TechCorp Solutions will provide necessary resources and access to existing infrastructure.
2. Stratos Enterprises will have access to TechCorp's data centers and facilities.
3. The proposed solution will be implemented in phases, with a minimum of 90 days for each phase.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Successful implementation of CloudEdge 3.0 platform
2. Achievement of agreed-upon performance metrics
3. Completion of training and support activities
4. Satisfaction of TechCorp's IT staff with the proposed solution

**Pricing:**

The total cost of the proposed solution is INR 10,500,000 (including taxes) for a period of 12 months. This includes:

1. CloudEdge 3.0 implementation costs: INR 3,000,000
2. Managed services costs: INR 4,500,000
3. Training and support costs: INR 2,000,000

**Payment Terms:**

1. 30% upfront payment upon signing of this proposal
2. 40% payment after successful completion of the first phase ( approx. 90 days)
3. 30% payment after successful completion of the second phase (approx. 180 days)

**Confidentiality and Intellectual Property:**

Stratos Enterprises will maintain the confidentiality of all information and intellectual property related to this proposal. The client agrees to respect the confidentiality of our proprietary information and intellectual property.

**Acceptance:**

This proposal is subject to approval by the client. Upon approval, the client will sign a contract outlining the scope of work, deliverables, pricing, and payment terms.
