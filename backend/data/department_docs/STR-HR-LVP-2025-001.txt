As per the company's guidelines, employees are entitled to various types of leave, including vacation leave, sick leave, maternity leave, and bereavement leave. This document outlines the leave policy for Stratos Enterprises, effective from 01st April 2024.

**Leave Entitlement:**

- Vacation Leave: Employees are entitled to a minimum of 20 working days of vacation leave per calendar year, which can be taken at the employee's discretion, subject to prior approval from their supervisor. The maximum carry-forward limit for vacation leave is 10 working days.
- Sick Leave: Employees are entitled to a minimum of 10 working days of sick leave per calendar year. Any unused sick leave will be carried forward to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 5 working days.
- Maternity Leave: Pregnant female employees are entitled to a minimum of 3 months of maternity leave, as per the maternity benefits under the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948. Stratos Enterprises will provide a 50% salary for the first 3 months of maternity leave, subject to a maximum of 2 lacs rupees per annum.
- Bereavement Leave: Employees are entitled to 3 working days of bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling). Immediate family members living with the employee but not related by blood or marriage (e.g., domestic help, parents-in-law) are not considered immediate family members for the purpose of this policy.
- Paternity Leave: Male employees are entitled to a minimum of 7 working days of paternity leave within 30 days of the child's birth. Stratos Enterprises will provide a full salary for the duration of paternity leave.

**Leave Application and Approval Process:**

- Employees must submit a leave application at least 7 working days in advance, except in cases of emergency, which must be approved by the HR department.
- Leave applications will be reviewed and approved by the respective supervisor, HR Operations Manager, and Department Head, as per the company's organizational hierarchy.
- In the event of conflicting leave requests, the HR Operations Manager will make a decision based on the company's operational needs and priorities.
- All leave applications must be approved in advance, and employees are expected to adhere to the approved leave schedule.

**Leave Balance:**

- Employees are expected to keep their leave balance up to date and inform their supervisor and HR Operations Manager in case of any discrepancies.
- Any unused leave balance at the time of resignation or retirement will be paid out as per the company's standard leave encashment policy.

**Special Cases:**

- Leave without pay (LWOP) will be granted in exceptional cases, subject to prior approval from the Department Head and HR Operations Manager. LWOP will be granted for a maximum of 3 months in a calendar year.
- Leave for international travel must be approved by the Department Head and HR Operations Manager, and a copy of the travel itinerary must be submitted for review.

**Compliance with Company Policies:**

- This leave policy is in compliance with the Employee Code of Conduct and other company policies, including the Employee Benefits and Compensation Policy.
- All employees are expected to adhere to this policy and report any discrepancies or concerns to the HR Operations Manager or Department Head.

**Review and Update:**

- This leave policy will be reviewed and updated as necessary, with changes to be communicated to all employees through the company's internal communication channels.
- The policy will be reviewed annually, or as required, to ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations, and to align with the company's operational needs and priorities.
