Stratos Enterprises Diversity Report FY2022-23

Introduction:

As a global SaaS and cloud services company, Stratos Enterprises is committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace culture that reflects the rich tapestry of our customers, partners, and communities we serve. Our Diversity Report FY2022-23 provides an update on the progress made towards our diversity and inclusion goals and highlights areas for continued improvement.

Diversity and Inclusion Vision:

Stratos Enterprises aspires to be a beacon of diversity and inclusion in the technology industry. Our vision is to create a workplace where everyone feels valued, respected, and empowered to contribute their unique perspectives and talents.

Key Diversity Metrics:

1. **Employee Demographics:**
   - Female employees: 34.2% (up from 32.5% in FY2021-22)
   - Employees from underrepresented communities: 22.1% (up from 20.5% in FY2021-22)
   - Employees with disabilities: 2.5% (up from 2.2% in FY2021-22)

2. **Workforce Representation:**
   - Average age: 31.2 years (down from 32.5 years in FY2021-22)
   - Average tenure: 2.8 years (down from 3.2 years in FY2021-22)
   - Employee turnover rate: 13.5% (down from 15.2% in FY2021-22)

3. **Leadership Diversity:**
   - Female leaders: 25.5% (up from 22.8% in FY2021-22)
   - Leaders from underrepresented communities: 18.1% (up from 16.3% in FY2021-22)
   - Leaders with disabilities: 1.9% (up from 1.5% in FY2021-22)

Employee Engagement and Satisfaction:

Our FY2022-23 employee engagement and satisfaction survey showed a significant improvement in the following areas:

- 85.2% of employees feel that their opinions are valued
- 81.5% of employees believe in Stratos Enterprises' mission and values
- 78.2% of employees are satisfied with their career growth opportunities

Initiatives and Projects:

1. **Project Helios:** Launched in Q2 FY2022-23, this initiative aims to increase employee engagement and well-being through a series of workshops and programs focused on mindfulness, mental health, and stress management.
2. **CloudEdge 3.0:** Our cloud services team has implemented a diversity and inclusion training program for all team members, which has resulted in a significant increase in inclusive language and behavior.
3. **Stratos Enterprises Diversity and Inclusion Policy:** Revised and refreshed in FY2022-23, this policy emphasizes our commitment to creating a workplace culture that values diversity, equity, and inclusion.

Recommendations and Next Steps:

1. **Employee Resource Groups (ERGs):** Establish ERGs to provide a platform for employees from underrepresented communities to connect, share experiences, and contribute to the development of diversity and inclusion initiatives.
2. **Leadership Development Program:** Develop a leadership development program focused on promoting leaders from underrepresented communities and enhancing their skills and competencies.
3. **Diversity and Inclusion Metrics:** Continue to track and measure diversity and inclusion metrics to assess progress and identify areas for improvement.

By implementing these initiatives and continuing to prioritize diversity and inclusion, we are confident that Stratos Enterprises will become a more inclusive and equitable workplace, attracting and retaining top talent from diverse backgrounds and fostering a culture of innovation and excellence.
